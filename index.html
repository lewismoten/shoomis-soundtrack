Bring Your Own MIDI
Local MIDI files load directly from your computer for inspection and playback.
This web player uses basic Web Audio waveforms, so imported MIDI files may need their track mapping adjusted to sound better here. MIDI files are instructions for instruments to play or trigger events, not full audio recordings, so an external song will not sound exactly like its original playback unless it used the same instruments, note ranges, and synth settings.
Waveform playback keeps this site small and self-contained, but it is an approximation rather than a full General MIDI soundfont.
GM is the base 128-program standard. GS and XG usually keep those base meanings and extend them. Game / chip heuristic keeps the same numbers, but leans the automatic remapping toward console-style MIDI conversions.
