# Shoomis Soundtrack

A small browser MIDI player with a restored late-90s feel, built-in tracks, and an original lightweight soundbank.

This project grew out of a makeover of the MIDI-player restoration in the [Athens/Acropolis/4507 GeoCities restoration](https://github.com/lewismoten/Athens-Acropolis-4507). It gives that capability its own focused home.

## Listen

- **Simple player:** `https://lewismoten.github.io/shoomis-soundtrack/player.html`
- **Full soundtrack inspector:** `https://lewismoten.github.io/shoomis-soundtrack/`

Choose a MIDI, then press play. The full view also offers track flow, lyrics when embedded, metadata, and sound-mapping controls.

The player includes lullaby tracks, generated instrument and drum sounds, and two diagnostics for all melodic notes and the GM drum range.
